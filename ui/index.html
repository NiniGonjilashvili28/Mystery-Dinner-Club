    How an evening unfolds

    1. Choose a city and a night At least three days ahead, so the restaurant can prepare your table.
    2. Pick a mood and a budget Luxury, café, wine bar, bar or dessert, or leave it to chance. From 30€ a head.
    3. We reserve, and keep it secret The restaurant knows you're coming. You don't know where yet.
    4. Three hours before, the seal breaks Name, address, your table number, and any special menu or discount.
    5. Dine, then rate it Your visits build a history, so next time we can send you somewhere new.
